Computer Science · Ph.D.

Nadav Z. Cohen

Ph.D. student at Reichman University with Prof. Ariel Shamir. I build generative models for 2D and 3D — algorithm-driven art forms and better tools for the game and animation industries.

Currently I'm interning at Adobe in Seattle as a Research Scientist in the Creative Intelligence group, mentored by Aharon Hertzman and Eli Shechtman. Previously, I was a Visiting Researcher in the IGARASHI Lab at the University of Tokyo, working with I-Chao Shen and Takeo Igarashi.

Off-screen: painting, nature photography, gaming, and time with family and friends.
